Frequently Asked Questions

How do I send?
On the home screen, tap the other device, choose photos and videos, then tap Send. You can also select items in the Photos app, tap Share and choose PairCarry.
How do I receive?
Just open PairCarry to receive. Tap the receive button at the top right for Receive Mode: the screen stays awake so others find you more easily. The first time a device sends to you, you’re asked whether to accept.
What if I can’t find the other device?
Make sure PairCarry is open on both devices, Wi-Fi is turned on for both (no network connection needed), and PairCarry is allowed to use Local Network in Settings. Keep the devices close together with their screens on.
Do I need internet or an account?
No. The two devices connect directly over peer-to-peer Wi-Fi, without any server and without using cellular data.
What if a transfer is interrupted?
When the connection comes back, the transfer resumes where it left off. If it still fails, open it in History and tap Resend to send only the items that didn’t make it.
Is quality reduced?
No. Photos, Live Photos and videos are sent as their original files, with the capture date and location kept.
